Booking

Drag across the slot you want. That's it.

Every room's week on a single screen. Select the time, name the meeting, and it becomes a real Google Calendar event with the room attached.

Try to take a slot that's already taken and it won't let you — it offers the next free window in that room instead, so you're not left hunting manually.

What makes it different

It doesn't wait for you to go looking. It comes to you.

The system reads your meetings for the next seven days, finds the ones with no room assigned, and tells you which rooms are free at exactly that time.

The system adapts to how your team already works instead of asking them to change.

Slack

A direct message to the person it concerns.

Everything arrives as a DM. There's no extra channel for anyone to mute.

  • On booking Confirmation to whoever booked and to the attendees, with the room and the time.
  • 5 minutes before it starts A reminder, so nobody is late to their own meeting.
  • 5 minutes before it ends A nudge to wrap up and free the room for whoever is next.
  • 8:00 AM A daily digest: what's scheduled and what's still open.

Slack is optional. The planner and the availability screen work without it, and it can be connected later without touching anything else.

Front desk

A live availability screen.

A second view built to sit on a monitor by the rooms or at reception: what's busy now, what frees up and when. It refreshes itself.
