Booking

Drag across the slot you want. That's it.

Scroll the week across every room, select the time, name the meeting — and it becomes a real Google Calendar event with the room attached.

Try to take a slot that's already taken and it won't let you — it offers the next free window in that room instead, so you're not left hunting manually. Ask for quick suggestions and it lists every open gap across the week.

Each room carries its capacity, so a pair doesn't take the ten-seater. Adding a client or a candidate is one field — they get the invite like any other guest.

The difference

It doesn't wait for you to go looking. It comes to you.

The system reads your meetings for the next seven days, finds the ones with no room assigned, and tells you which rooms are free at exactly that time.

After booking

Your day, and one click to give a room back.

Today's bookings sit under the planner: what you reserved, which room, what time. Cancel from there and the room is free for everyone immediately — no hunting through a calendar for the event you made this morning.

And when a meeting ends early, the room should not stay blocked until five o'clock. Five minutes before each one is due to finish, whoever booked it gets a nudge — so the room goes back into circulation instead of sitting empty with the door closed.

No new habits

It writes to your calendar, and it reads it back.

Every booking is written straight into Google Calendar as a real event, with the room as a guest. Nothing sits in a separate system waiting to fall out of sync — the calendar is the system.

So it runs both ways. Invite a room from Google Calendar and it appears in the planner and fires the same notifications. Delete the event there and the room frees up here. In practice people don't open another app — they schedule from the calendar they already live in, and that keeps working.

Slack

A direct message to the person it concerns.

Everything arrives as a DM. There's no extra channel for anyone to mute.

  • On booking Confirmation to whoever booked and to the attendees, with the room and the time.
  • 5 min before start A reminder, so nobody is late to their own meeting.
  • 5 min before end A nudge to wrap up and free the room for whoever is next.
  • 08:00 daily A digest: what's scheduled and what's still open.

Slack is optional. The planner and the availability screen work without it, and it can be connected later without touching anything else.

Front desk

A live availability screen.

A second view built to sit on a monitor by the rooms or at reception: what's busy now, what frees up and when. It refreshes itself, so nobody has to touch it.

It answers the question people ask by walking up and trying the handle — including visitors, who have no access to any of this.
